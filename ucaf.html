      How to avoid deductions

      From the NPHIES line reports of July and August 2026 · open for the rules every doctor and receptionist should know
      Reason the insurer gaveLost (SAR)What to do
      Diagnosis does not fit the test10,903Every test needs a diagnosis that explains it. CBC with a cold only was rejected 58 times (3,708 SAR); also glucose, hepatitis, lipase, ferritin, cortisol and liver tests without a matching diagnosis.
      Not clinically justified4,476HbA1c only with a diabetes (E10–E14) or prediabetes (R73) code — 1,608 SAR lost. Ceftriaxone and other antibiotics only with an infection code.
      Same item twice on the same day2,800Enter each medicine once with the full quantity. Ceftriaxone vials entered as 3–6 separate lines lost 2,202 SAR.
      Not compliant with the contract2,496Prices above the contract for some companies (Nesma, GC Interwell, Sepco…) and items outside the contract (Neodex drops). Management: check the price list with each insurer.
      New consultation within 14 days1,085Reception: a patient back to the same doctor within 14 days is registered as a free follow-up, not a new consultation.
      Medicine not in the formulary872Reparil and Remox were rejected most. Prescribe a covered alternative.
      Chief complaint missing or too short661On a new visit, ultrasound, X-ray, IV fluids and IV injections need a complaint that explains them. On a follow-up, "for injection" or "for dressing" is enough.
      Co-pay not collected231Reception: collect the patient's share before the visit.
      Wrong medicine dose148An injection given in the clinic is one day of supply, not 7 (ondansetron injections were rejected for this).
      Antibiotic injection not justified (July)4,561Ceftriaxone (Enoxirt) injections were rejected 130 of 161 times with J22, J06, J03 or asthma. Give an injection only for pneumonia, UTI / pyelonephritis, cellulitis or abscess — otherwise an antibiotic by mouth.
      Nebulizer without asthma (July)679Nebulizer sessions and Pulmicort ampoules need asthma, wheeze (R06.2), bronchiolitis or COPD — cough or chest infection alone was rejected.
      Test repeated too soon (July)568Liver, lipids, glucose, urinalysis and X-ray repeated within days were rejected as too frequent.
      Preauthorization not obtained (July)507IV fluids and IV injections for Marine Innovation, Saudi Arabic Computer Systems and Zamil Central need approval first.
      Result missing or wrong sex (July)752Attach the ultrasound report to the claim. A pelvic ultrasound for a male patient was rejected.
